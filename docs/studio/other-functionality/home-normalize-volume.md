# HOME → Normalize volume

The ‘Normalize volume’ command, available in the ‘Tools’ group on the HOME tab, makes all sounds in the quiz play equally loud. QuizXpress measures how loud each sound is and sets its loudness gain towards a target loudness you choose. The sound files themselves aren't changed.

![Normalize volume on the HOME ribbon tab](../../assets/images/normalize-home-tools.webp){ width="220" loading=lazy }

See [Normalizing the volume](../adding-multimedia-content/sound.md#normalizing-the-volume) for a full description, including how to normalize a single sound.
