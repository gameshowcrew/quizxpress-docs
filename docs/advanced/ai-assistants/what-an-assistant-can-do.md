# What an assistant can do

An assistant works with the quiz that is open in Quiz Studio, and does what you ask in plain language. This page lists what it can do, so you know what to ask for.

## Opening and saving

- Start a new quiz (with a 16:9 resolution unless you ask for another) or open an existing one.
- Save the quiz, or save a copy under another name.
- Fill in the quiz details, such as the description and your name as author.

If you want to start with a quiz that has no content yet, ask for "an empty quiz": the assistant starts without a leftover first slide.

## Finding your way around

- Give an overview of all slides, one line each: number, type and question text. This is the quickest way for an assistant to find a slide.
- Show a slide in Studio, so you see what it is talking about.
- Look at a slide as a picture, to check the result of its changes.

## Creating slides

An assistant can create all slide types that the [Quiz Wizard](../../studio/user-interface-overview/the-quiz-wizard.md) can, with the content you give it:

- Multiple choice questions (with one or more correct answers), ordered questions, and open, number, letter and text questions
- Audience response questions
- Pairing questions with pictures, and hotspot questions on a picture
- Wager slides and end-of-round slides
- Picture rounds and music rounds, from pictures and sound files on your computer
- Speed rounds, [Last Man Standing](../../studio/slide-and-game-formats/last-man-standing.md) rounds, [Trivia Ladder](../../studio/slide-and-game-formats/trivia-ladder-round.md) and [Trivia Board](../../studio/slide-and-game-formats/trivia-board-round.md) rounds
- [Bingo rounds](../../studio/slide-and-game-formats/bingo-round.md), including the questions of a Trivia Bingo
- Banners, test questions, demographic groups, majority rules questions and Jeopardy rounds
- [Mini games](../../studio/mini-games/index.md)

For each of these it can choose the style, the answer time and the points.

## Changing slides

An assistant can read and change almost every property you see in Studio's properties panel: the question and answer texts, which answer is correct, points, answer time, style, background (also from a picture file or web address), pictures, notes and much more. It can change many slides at once, for example "set the answer time of all questions to 20 seconds".

It can also reorder, copy and delete slides and change the layout of a slide, for example from four to six answers.

## Checking and finishing

- **Check the quiz** for mistakes, the same check Studio does before saving. It finds, for example, questions without a correct answer, and Bingo questions that have the same answer, which would make the cards unclear.
- **Shuffle the answers** of all or some questions. The correct answers move with them.
- **Import slides** from another quiz.
- **Export a PDF** of the quiz for the quizmaster, with the correct answers marked.
- **Create and edit styles** (.qxstyle files) for your quizzes.

## Examples of what to ask

- "Make a 10 question pub quiz about the 90s, multiple choice with four answers, style 'Neon Night Arcade', 20 seconds per question, and finish with an end-of-round slide."
- "Add a picture round with the three pictures in the folder C:\Pictures\Cities."
- "Build a Trivia Bingo with 25 questions about general knowledge, simple formatting, and make sure no two answers are the same."
- "Read through all questions, correct spelling mistakes and tell me which questions look wrong."
- "Add a Rope Rumble mini game after every 10th question."

!!! tip
    The more precisely you describe what you want, the better the result. Tell the assistant which style, how many answers and how much time you want, and ask it to show you the first slides before it makes the rest.

## What an assistant can't do

- Control Quiz Live, Director or Setup. It works in Quiz Studio only.
- Do things that only exist in Studio's menus and dialogs, for example generating paper Bingo cards. Do those yourself.
- Judge how a slide looks as well as you can. It can look at a picture of the slide, but check the result yourself before you use a quiz in a show.
