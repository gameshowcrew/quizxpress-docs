# Billboard

A ‘Billboard’ quiz slide is a quiz slide with no interaction from the players. It is just a static display typically used for announcements or explanations. You can design a billboard quiz slide just like you design a normal question, making use of texts, colors, pictures, sounds and videos.

Useful applications of a billboard are:

- Announcements (rounds/finishing of rounds/intermediate score announcements)

- Further explanation of the correct answer to an earlier question

- Marketing information or sales messages

- Show a preview of a multiple-choice question to come so the audience can prepare.

- Show a video or image before a question that the audience needs to watch carefully.
