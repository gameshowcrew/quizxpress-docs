# What's new in 8.6

The highlights of QuizXpress 8.6. The complete list of changes, including all smaller improvements and fixes, is in the release notes: see [Updates and version numbering](installation/updates-and-version-numbering.md).

## Let an AI assistant build your quiz

Quiz Studio can now be controlled by an AI assistant such as Claude. Tell it what quiz you want, and it creates the slides, fills in the questions and answers, sets styles and points, checks the quiz for mistakes and saves it, in the Quiz Studio window you have open. It is switched off by default; you turn it on in the Options, on the 'AI Completion' tab. See [AI assistants](advanced/ai-assistants/index.md).

## New question types and mini games

- [Pairing question](studio/slide-and-game-formats/pairing-question.md): players match items from two lists, for example a country and its flag. A hotspot question, where players select the right area on a picture, is new too.
- [Rope Rumble](studio/mini-games/rope-rumble.md): two teams pull the rope by buzzing as fast as they can.

## Easier to design with

- [Object list](studio/user-interface-overview/object-list-view.md): select and edit the items on a slide, even when many overlap.
- Collapse and expand rounds, and make the slide thumbnails larger or smaller, in the [thumbnails panel](studio/user-interface-overview/thumbnails-panel.md).
- A new properties panel that replaces the technical [properties grid](studio/user-interface-overview/advanced-properties-grid.md) (you can switch back to the grid).
- [Find and replace](studio/other-functionality/find-text-in-questions-and-answers.md) in the whole quiz.
- [Packing a quiz](studio/other-functionality/packing-and-unpacking-a-quiz.md) now trims and compresses sounds and videos, so packed quizzes become much smaller.

## Easier to run a show

- [Manual points](live/quizxpress-director/the-manual-scoring-view.md) in Director: quickly give points to one or more players.
- Profile pictures of players using mobile or web keypads show in the scoreboards and other screens.
